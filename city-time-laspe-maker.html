Events

Calamities that strike the city

An event strikes at the current year. A naval siege appears on maps with a sea: a fleet sails in, bombards the shore and lands troops. A ceremony fills the streets with a parade, music and, in later eras, fireworks. The clock stops while it plays out, then speeds back up. Fires only burn wooden, thatched and tent buildings; stone, brick, earth and stucco survive. Fires, riots and natural disasters are rebuilt automatically. Sieges and sacks leave ruins until you clear them or bury them under raised ground. Monsoons appear for South Asian cities, hurricanes for Caribbean and Mesoamerican ones, and tornadoes for Central European and Great Plains ones.

Map

Builder bot

Describe it, and it lays out streets

How it works

Streets first, buildings follow

The map is about 5 mi across and starts as untouched land. Trees are cleared as you build. Draw streets and houses with fenced yards fill in along them a few at a time, spreading outward from the oldest part of town; far-off streets wait until the town grows toward them, and the old core ends up the densest. Trails, dirt roads and cobbles are upgraded over the centuries on their own. Farmland, quays, quarries, ports and warehouses are in the Production tab. Walls open into gates where streets cross.

Two fingers pan, pinch and turn the map. The Move tool drags it. Menus close when you tap the map.
